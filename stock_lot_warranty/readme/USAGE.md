You can define the customer warranty period can be defined in Inventory > Products > Products
and inside a product, in the "Sales" tab under the "Warranty" section.

The vendor warranty period can be defined in Inventory > Products > Products and inside a product,
in the "Purchase" tab. You need to specify, in the supplier info table, the "Period".

The vendor warranty end date is computed based on the receipt date and the vendor warranty period. The customer
warranty end date is computed based on the delivery date and the customer warranty period.
