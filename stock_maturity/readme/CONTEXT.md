In food industries (and maybe in other sectors involving manufacturing as well) some
manufacturing procesess involve that a product might not be ready to deliver until
it passes a final madurity process.

So the product is stocked, but it isn't available until a certain period.

Examples of these could be:

 - Fruit that is store in chambers until is ripe enough.
 - Dry-aged meat.
 - Cheeses of all kinds.
