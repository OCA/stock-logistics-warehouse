Protect critical production data while allowing departments to maintain
their own products and warehouse information.

This creates a secure separation between industrial controls and sales
operations, providing essential support for Good Manufacturing Practices
(GMP) compliance by ensuring proper access controls and data integrity
for critical manufacturing information.

**Manufacturing Company Example:**

*Production Department* (Full Access): - Manages master production data
and main warehouse configs - Controls all product specs, bills of
materials, and inventory - Ensures data integrity for critical
manufacturing info

*Sales Department* (Limited Access): - Creates kits or assembled
products for their sales needs - Maintains some warehouse locations,
such as distribution centers - Cannot access or modify master production
data - Works independently within their designated scope

Configuration
=============

To configure this module:

**User Groups:**

1. Go to *Settings > Users & Companies > Users*
2. For users managing non-restricted products (e.g., subsidiary users):

   -  Assign them to the **Product Creation** group under Inventory

3. For users managing all products (e.g., production department):

   -  Assign them to the **Product Creation - Restricted** group under
      Inventory

**Product Categories:**

1. Go to *Inventory > Configuration > Product Categories*
2. Open a category that should be restricted (e.g., production master
   data)
3. Check the **Restricted Access** field and save

**Warehouses:**

1. Go to *Inventory > Configuration > Warehouses*
2. Open a warehouse that should be restricted (e.g., main production)
3. Check the **Restricted Access** field and save

**Note:** The **Product Creation** group is useful when you remove
existing product creation access from App Administrator groups to
implement tighter security controls. This allows you to grant specific
product creation permissions without giving full administrative access.

Usage
=====

**Standard Users:**

-  *Products:* Create and edit products in your assigned categories
-  *Bills of Materials:* Create and modify BOMs for products you can
   access, work normally with manufacturing specs for allowed items,
   error messages appear for restricted BOMs
-  *Warehouses:* Manage inventory locations in your authorized
   warehouses, create and adjust stock locations as needed, cannot
   access restricted warehouse configurations

**Restricted Privileged Users:**

-  *Products:* Work with all products without restrictions
-  *Bills of Materials:* Create and modify all BOMs with full control
-  *Warehouses:* Manage all warehouse locations and configurations
-  *Security Settings:* Modify restricted access flags on categories and
   warehouses
