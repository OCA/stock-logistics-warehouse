Protect critical production data while allowing departments to maintain
their own products and warehouse information.

This creates a secure separation between industrial controls and sales
operations, providing essential support for Good Manufacturing Practices
(GMP) compliance by ensuring proper access controls and data integrity
for critical manufacturing information.

**Manufacturing Company Example:**

*Production Department* (Full Access): - Manages master production data
and main warehouse configs - Controls all product specs, bills of
materials, and inventory - Ensures data integrity for critical
manufacturing info

*Sales Department* (Limited Access): - Creates kits or assembled
products for their sales needs - Maintains some warehouse locations,
such as distribution centers - Cannot access or modify master production
data - Works independently within their designated scope
