Usage
=====

Once installed, when a stock move is performed, the system will automatically update the available warehouses and locations for the related product.

You can update information for all products by going to Inventory > Reporting > Update Product Stock Available. This will recalculate the available warehouses and locations for all products based on their current stock levels.

You can then use this information to filter or group products based on their availability in specific warehouses or locations.
