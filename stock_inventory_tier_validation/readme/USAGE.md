1.  Create an inventory adjustment, press *Begin Adjustments* and enter the
    counted quantities.
2.  Press *Request Validation*. While the validation is in progress, the
    counted quantities cannot be changed.
3.  Once the reviewers have validated the adjustment, press *Set to Done*. The
    counted quantities are applied and the stock moves are created.

If the adjustment is rejected, or the stock has moved since the count, press
*Restart Validation*, correct the counted quantities and request the
validation again.
