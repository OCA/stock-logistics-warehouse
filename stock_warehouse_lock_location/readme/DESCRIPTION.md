Warehouses get a **Lock Location**, next to the standard stock and quality
control locations, holding the goods that are locked: present in the
warehouse, but not to be shipped unless explicitly requested.

The location is created together with the other warehouse locations, and for
the existing warehouses when the module is installed. Its goods, sub-locations
included, are left out of the forecasted report, so they do not make a waiting
delivery show as available.
